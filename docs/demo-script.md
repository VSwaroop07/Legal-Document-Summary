# Five-minute demo script

## 0:00-0:45 — Sign in

1. Open the client and register a demo account.
2. Log in and point out the responsive navbar, disclaimer, and protected dashboard.

## 0:45-1:45 — Upload and process a document

1. Drag a text-based legal PDF into the upload card.
2. Show the progress indicator and the ready status.
3. Open the document and point out page count and metadata.

## 1:45-2:45 — Generate and explain the summary

1. Select **Generate summary**.
2. Show the structured sections: background, issues, arguments, reasoning, judgment, and takeaways.
3. Select a sentence and use the **Explain** popup.
4. Point out the disclaimer and grounded explanation sections.

## 2:45-3:45 — Ask questions with citations

1. Open **Ask questions**.
2. Ask “What was the final decision?”
3. Expand **Sources**, click a citation, and show page/chunk details.
4. Ask a follow-up such as “Why did the court decide that?”

## 3:45-4:35 — Browse news

1. Open **News** and show category counts, search, pagination, and AI-generated labels.
2. Use **Explain with AI** on an article.
3. Open **Read original** in a new tab.

## 4:35-5:00 — Safety and polish

1. Show that logout removes access to `/dashboard`.
2. Visit an invalid route to show the 404 page.
3. Mention that demo news can be loaded with `npm run seed:demo --prefix server`.

The output is for understanding only and is not legal advice.
